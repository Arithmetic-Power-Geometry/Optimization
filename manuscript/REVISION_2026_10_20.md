### Corrected series-path probability proof
Let J be the uniformly selected failed edge among n edges and I the edge chosen for the first and only restoration. Before any restoration all allowed full-path observations return DOWN, so I and J are independent. If the policy attempts a restoration, the exact calculation is
```latex
\begin{align}
\Pr(\mathrm{success})
&=\Pr(I=J)\\
&=\sum_{i=1}^{n}\Pr(I=i)\Pr(J=i)\\
&=\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^{n}\Pr(I=i)=\frac1n.
\end{align}
```
Any fixed-edge restoration achieves the optimum. A policy that declines restoration may have lower success. This repairs the malformed proof text in the earlier PDF; it does **not** establish a novel information-theoretic theorem.
