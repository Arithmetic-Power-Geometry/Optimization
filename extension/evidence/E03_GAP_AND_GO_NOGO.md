# E03 — Gap assessment and gate
Date: 2026-10-20

## Rejected originality assertions (NO-GO)
1. Decision sufficiency / overlapping repair-valid regions: already DRD/HEC.
2. Cost-sensitive or noisy active tests: EC2/ECED.
3. End-to-end failure indistinguishability: network tomography.
4. Information discovery dependent on decisions: DDID.
5. Repair-information interleaving under uncertain network damage: progressive network recovery.
6. Belief-set recursion as a new computational method: POMDP theory.
7. Simple n-edge 1/n symmetry identity as a stand-alone new theorem: unsupported novelty.

## Narrow open question (NOT validated gap)
Physically restricted, intervention-dependent path accessibility with an irreversible repair budget and robust terminal service constraints MAY yield a graph-family-specific lower bound or algorithm. A difference in model vocabulary is not a demonstrated literature gap. Prior art could subsume it.

## Formal gate for E04–E08
- Specify all states, graph modifications, monitor placements, feasible tests and outcomes; prohibit direct edge inspection unless granted to every policy.
- Specify success under robust and Bayesian conventions separately.
- Construct minimal adversarial countermodels and a parameterized family, with correct resource accounting.
- Compare published theorem assumptions and conclusions, not merely abstracts, especially progressive recovery and DDID.
- Show one substantive nontrivial separation/hardness/approximation theorem beyond a direct DRD/POMDP specialization.
- Independent proof audit must pass before labeling any result original.
