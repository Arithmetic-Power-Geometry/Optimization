# E08 — External primary theorem collision audit
Date: 2026-10-20. Evidence depth: primary article and available publisher-hosted extended proof; not an exhaustive systematic review.

1. Javdani et al., AISTATS/PMLR 33 (2014), https://proceedings.mlr.press/v33/javdani14.pdf. Paper states the version-space-in-one-decision-region criterion; CMU extended PDF https://publications.ri.cmu.edu/storage/publications/pub_files/2014/4/javdani14hec_extended.pdf, Section 7.2 Theorem 1, proves equivalence of DRD and HEC stopping. E05's R_i={(i,L),(i,R)} are disjoint decision regions and strictly simpler than the published overlapping-region case. The CMU technical report is an extended version of the same publication, NOT an additional independent work.
2. Vayanos, Georghiou & Yu, Management Science 72(2):1509–1528 (2026 issue; published online June 2025), DOI 10.1287/mnsc.2021.00160, https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/mnsc.2021.00160. Publisher abstract explicitly states two- and multistage robust optimization with information-discovery timing controlled by prior investments and gives finite reformulations. Thus repair-enables-probing in E06 is not generically novel. arXiv:2004.08490 is the same research work, not another distinct study.
3. Golovin, Krause & Ray, NeurIPS 2010 EC2: https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2010/hash/1e6e0a04d20f50967c64dac2d639a577-Abstract.html. Equivalence-class identification and active tests predate E05.

## Boundary of this audit
The exact DRD/HEC theorem was compared at theorem level. For remaining works, publication statements, abstracts and scope were compared, not independently reconstructed complete published proofs. No exhaustive forward-citation mining or global 64-work verification. Accordingly the evidence supports a conservative NO-GO, not a universal proof that no possible novel result exists.
